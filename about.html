07 / FOUNDER
Gowri Priya Yadav Founder & Creative Director, RAREVO

"I didn't want to just build websites. I wanted to build brands people could actually hold, click, and scroll through."

Gowri Priya Yadav founded RAREVO out of a simple frustration: agencies that built a nice site and disappeared, leaving the rest of the brand — the social presence, the marketing, the physical product — to figure itself out. She started with web design, added social media and marketing as clients kept asking for more, and later launched RAREVO's own bag line to prove the same design thinking works off-screen too.

She still leads every brand strategy personally, and the studio's standard hasn't changed since day one: nothing ships unless it's built around the client's actual goals, not a template.
