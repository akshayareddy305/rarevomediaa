FOUNDER PROFILE

"I didn't want to just build websites. I wanted to build brands people could actually hold, click, and scroll through."

Gowri Priya Yadav is the founder and creative director of RAREVO. She started the studio in 2022 as a web design studio for local brands that wanted something better than a template, and has grown it, one client request at a time, into four crafts under one name: websites, social media, marketing and RAREVO's own line of bags.

RAREVO began with a simple frustration: agencies that built a nice site and disappeared, leaving the rest of the brand to figure itself out. Gowri keeps the whole brand in view — the website, the social presence, the campaigns and the physical product — so nothing about a client's brand feels outsourced.

She still leads every brand strategy personally, and the studio's standard hasn't changed since day one: nothing ships unless it's built around the client's actual goals, not a template.
